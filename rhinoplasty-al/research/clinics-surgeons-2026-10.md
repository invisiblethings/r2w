# Rhinoplasty clinics and surgeons in Albania: research register (October 2026)

Internal working document. Not for publication. Nothing here is an audit result: it is the list of who to audit, what each one claims, and what is already on the public record. Allegations are recorded as allegations, with their status.

Raw notes with every source URL: `research/raw/01–05`.

## Summary

- **26 entities** found, all in Tirana (Hygeia and Salus are on the Tirana–Durrës road, Kashar). No rhinoplasty provider was found in Durrës, Vlorë, Shkodër, Korçë, Elbasan or Fier; this is a directory gap, not proof of absence.
- **2 providers** publish rhinoplasty on their own site with named surgeons and EUR prices: Hygeia Hospital and American Hospital.
- **5 providers** offer rhinoplasty with thinner evidence (patient reviews, self-descriptions): KEIT, Clinica DaVinci, Silur Clinic, Dermolife, Dental & Beauty.
- **14 entities** appear only in directory listings; rhinoplasty, surgeon and price are unconfirmed.
- **5 entities** are brokers, dental clinics or unrelated listings.
- **Surgeons:** 9 surgeons can be tied to rhinoplasty in Albania by name, most of them Turkish or Italian and probably visiting. Clinics that market "20–30+ Italian surgeons" (KEIT, DaVinci) do not name them publicly.
- **No public online register** of licensed clinics or of doctors was found. Verification has to go through the QKB business register (licence category II.6) and written confirmation from the Order of Physicians.
- **Prices:** published and reported prices cluster at **EUR 2,000–4,500** for primary rhinoplasty and **EUR 3,800–5,500** for revision (Hygeia). Italian press reports EUR 1,800–3,500.

## How to verify a clinic and a surgeon in Albania

- **Clinic licence:** health providers need a licence under Law 10 107/2009 art. 27 (fine 500,000 Lek). Categories under VKM 538/2009, category II.6: A.4 medical centre with micro-surgery, A.5 aesthetic centre offering medical service, B.1 hospital, B.2 day hospital (decided by the Ministry of Health). Check the clinic's legal name or NIPT in the [QKB entity search](https://qkb.gov.al/site/index.php/kerko-per-subjekt/) for an active II.6 licence; the non-official mirror qkb.atlas-studio.eu shows licence status per business. A rhinoplasty under general anaesthesia should sit in a B.1 or B.2 licence.
- **Surgeon licence:** Law 123/2014 requires Order of Physicians membership and an individual licence; the Order keeps the register but no public online search was found. Ask each surgeon for the Order licence number and specialist diploma, and request written confirmation from the Order.
- **Foreign surgeons:** home register (Italy: FNOMCeO Albo; Turkey: Ministry of Health / TPRECD) plus the authorisation to practise in Albania.
- **Inspections:** ISHSH inspects but does not license; it announced in March 2026 that plastic-surgery licensing is a 2026 priority.
- **Accreditation:** no public list of accredited institutions found.
