## How to verify a doctor in Albania
- Legal basis: Law No. 123/2014 on the Order of Physicians: no one may practise in public or private sector without Order membership and an individual practice licence issued by it; the Order keeps a register and can revoke licences; ethics code obliges registration. https://qsut.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Ligj-Nr.123-2014-Per-Urdhrin-e-Mjekeve-ne-Republiken-e-Shqiperise.pdf (amended by Normative Act 2/2024; Constitutional Court decision 58/2025).
- Public search tool: I could NOT find or confirm any public online doctor search on the Order's site. Guesses (umsh.al, urdhrimjekeve.al) do not resolve. The ministry portal https://mjeke.shendetesia.gov.al ("Mjeke per Shqiperine") is only a recruitment portal, no register. CPME lists the Order's address as Rr. "Belul Hatibi", Poliklinika Nr. 10, Tirana (may be outdated).
- Practical method: ask the doctor/clinic for the Order licence number and specialist diploma (plastic surgery or ORL), then request written confirmation from the Order of Physicians; check the clinic's facility licence with the Ministry of Health/ National Authority for Health Licensing; for foreign surgeons ask for home-country register number (e.g. Italy FNOMCeO Albo; Mustafa's Italian number is 20893) and confirm Albanian authorisation to practise; cross-check ISAPS directory (isaps.org find-a-surgeon, filter Albania).
- Follow-up recommended: fetch the clinics' own sites (gera tagani, keit.al, dermolife, chimera.al, maxclinique) and contact the Order directly.
