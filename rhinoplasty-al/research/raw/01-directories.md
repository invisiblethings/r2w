## Aggregator coverage
- WhatClinic says 11 rhinoplasty clinics in Albania. A search snippet said 8 clinics and 283 reviews; the live page says 472 reviews. Counts are inconsistent. Only about 11 of the 11 could be read across the two pages, and the Tirana plastic-surgery listing says 10, so some may not be seen.
- Bookimed: the Albania surgery page lists only Gremi Clinic. No Albanian rhinoplasty profiles were found.
- 123.clinic: lists only Austria Dental. It gives an average of EUR 3,600 (range 3,000 to 4,200), a lead-generation figure.
- Placidway Albania: lists only dental clinics (Excelentis, Dental Med Austria, Dental Estetik, Emerald Dental); no rhinoplasty provider named.
- Trustpilot: KEIT (4.8, 2,182 reviews) and Silur (4.2, 15 reviews). No Trustpilot page was found for the other clinics.
- RealSelf: one patient review (Sinani). The page returned 403.
- Not found: Doctoralia, topdoctors, medigo, qunomedical, flymedi, estheticon, treatmentabroad, clinicspots. Not found does not mean absent; these were only covered by a few search queries.
- Money.it gives EUR 1,800 to 3,500 for Albania (https://www.money.it/quanto-costa-rifarsi-il-naso-prezzi-della-rinoplastica); I did not fetch it.

## Notes & red flags
- No listing exists outside Tirana. Searches for Durres, Vlore, Shkoder, Korce, Elbasan and Fier found only a Durres dental clinic (Golden Dental). Absence is likely a directory-coverage gap and should be checked with local sources.
- The WhatClinic "ServiceScore" is a lead-activity metric (interactions), not a review rating. Do not compare it with star ratings.
- KEIT review counts conflict (428, 472, 2,062 and 2,326 interactions; Trustpilot 2,182). KEIT's WhatClinic rhinoplasty prices conflict (144,924 vs 326,079 Lek). Trustpilot has a 1-star complaint that the nose result differed from the image shown.
- Hygeia's page shows a "medically reviewed" date in December 2026, which is in the future, so its content metadata is unreliable. Its rating and review count are self-reported.
- Gen-o Time's WhatClinic address says "Istanbul", and Bookimed says it is not accepting new requests, so the Tirana branch may be defunct.
- Dental clinics (Austria Dental, International Dental Center) appear on rhinoplasty or plastic-surgery listings; treat as unverified for surgery.
- Surgery Abroad Specialist Tirana and Gaia Medical Solutions look like facilitators or brokers rather than surgical facilities, and Surgery Abroad's prices are about double the others.
- Dr. Sokol Isaraj has two different addresses across sources, and his reviews do not mention rhinoplasty.
- Surgeon names from review text, image alt text and filenames (Orsini, Arapi, Dervishaj, Giosci, Albiona) are unverified, and no credentials were checked in any registry.
- Dr. Tagani's Stanford and Beverly Hills claims come from the clinic's own description.
- The WhatClinic prices cited are mostly undated Lek figures from different pages for the same clinic (e.g. International Family Hospital 145,257 / 146,082 / 146,660), so treat them as approximate.
