## How profiles are made

Every clinic is assessed by the [published method](/methodology/). Before a profile is published, the clinic receives its findings and has 14 days to correct facts or reply.

## Correct a fact

Send the correction with evidence (a license document, a surgeon's registration, a price list) to clinics@rhinoplasty.al. We correct verified facts within 7 days and date the change on the profile.

## Reply to a finding

Your reply is printed on your profile, unedited except for length (up to 150 words) and personal data of patients.

## Request an audit

Clinics that are not yet listed can request an audit by writing to clinics@rhinoplasty.al. Requesting an audit is free and does not guarantee a ranking.

## Referral partnership

Clinics can pay a referral fee for consultation requests. Partners are labelled, and the fee does not affect the score or the ranking. [Advertiser disclosure](/advertiser-disclosure/)
