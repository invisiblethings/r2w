Rhinoplasty.al is an independent guide for people considering rhinoplasty in Albania. We are not a clinic and we do not perform surgery.

## What we do

- Check that clinics and surgeons are licensed.
- Survey what rhinoplasty costs in Albania, with written quotes and patients' invoices.
- Score clinics by a [published method](/methodology/) and print each clinic's reply.
- Publish guides on techniques, recovery and safety, written by medical writers and reviewed by a surgeon.

## What we will not do

- Sell rankings, or let payment change a score.
- Publish reviews or before and after photos we have not verified.
- Push you to book. A consultation request is free and you are under no obligation.

## How we are funded

Some clinics pay a referral fee when you request a consultation through us. [Advertiser disclosure](/advertiser-disclosure/)
