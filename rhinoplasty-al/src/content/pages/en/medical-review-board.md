Each guide about a procedure, recovery or risk is reviewed by a surgeon listed on this page, with their specialty, registration and a link to a public record of their credentials.

## Independence rules

- A reviewer never reviews a page that ranks or profiles a clinic they work for or with.
- Reviewers declare every clinic they have worked with in the past five years.
- Reviewers are paid per review, never per referral.
