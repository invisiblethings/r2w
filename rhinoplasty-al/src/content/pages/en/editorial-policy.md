## Who writes our guides

Guides are written by medical writers in English, Italian and Albanian. Each language is written by a native speaker, not machine-translated.

## Medical review

Every guide about a procedure, recovery or risk is reviewed by a surgeon before it is listed in search engines. The reviewer's name, credentials and review date appear at the top of the page. Pages awaiting review say so.

## Sources

Medical statements cite peer-reviewed research, professional bodies or official health sources. Prices cite our own survey or a dated, named source.

## Independence

Writers and reviewers do not know which clinics pay a referral fee, and commercial staff cannot edit guides or scores.

## Corrections

If you find an error, write to us through the [contact page](/contact/). Corrections are made within 7 days and dated on the page.

## Updates

Guides are reviewed at least once a year and whenever the evidence changes.
