The information on Rhinoplasty.al is for general information. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with a qualified surgeon who has examined you.

Every nose and every patient is different. Results, risks and recovery times described here are typical, not guaranteed.

If you have symptoms after surgery such as heavy bleeding, fever, increasing pain or difficulty breathing, contact your surgeon or local emergency services immediately.

If you find yourself thinking about your nose most of the day, or feel that no result would be good enough, talk to a doctor before deciding on surgery. These can be signs of body dysmorphic disorder, which surgery does not treat.
