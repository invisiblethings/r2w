## How we earn money

When you request a consultation through Rhinoplasty.al, we pass your request to the clinic you chose. Some clinics pay us a referral fee for these requests. These clinics are labelled **Partner** wherever they appear.

## What payment does not change

- **Scores and rankings.** Scores are calculated from the [published criteria](/methodology/). Whether a clinic pays is not an input.
- **Which clinics we list.** Every clinic that passes the license check can be listed, whether or not it pays.
- **What we write.** Writers and medical reviewers do not know which clinics pay.

## What you pay

Nothing. Requesting a consultation is free, and the price a clinic quotes you is the same whether you contact it directly or through us.

## Main ranking parameters

The main parameters that determine a clinic's ranking are its license status (a clinic must pass), surgeon credentials (25%), facility and anesthesia (20%), price transparency (15%), aftercare and revision policy (15%), patient evidence (15%) and international patient service (10%).
