Choosing a surgeon for your nose is a decision you live with every day. This guide helps you make it with facts: which clinics in Albania hold a valid license, what a rhinoplasty really costs once everything is included, how long you need to stay, and what to do if something goes wrong after you fly home.

**How this guide works**

- **Licenses are checked.** A clinic without a license we can confirm is never ranked.
- **Prices are surveyed.** We request written quotes and collect patients' invoices, and every price shows when it was collected.
- **Scores follow a published method.** Every clinic profile shows the score for each criterion. [How we rank](/methodology/)
- **Clinics can reply.** Every clinic sees its findings before they are published, and its reply is printed on its profile.
- **Payment never buys a score.** Some clinics pay a referral fee. They are labelled, and the fee does not change their score. [Advertiser disclosure](/advertiser-disclosure/)
