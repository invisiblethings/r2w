This page collects the rhinoplasty prices for Albania that clinics, media and listing sites have published, each with its source and date. **These are published or reported prices, not our survey.** Our own survey, based on written quotes for the same standard cases, is in progress and will be added when it is complete. A published range is a starting point. It is not an offer. Your quote depends on your nose, the technique and what is included.

## What do clinics publish about their own prices?

Two Tirana hospitals publish rhinoplasty prices with named procedures on their own sites. Both pages are undated, so treat them as current when the page was read for this guide (October 2026), not as a price list you can hold them to.

| Hygeia Hospital Tirana, own site | Undated | Primary €2,000–3,800 | Surgeon, anaesthesia, facility, follow-up during stay; hotel and flights extra |
| Hygeia Hospital Tirana, own site | Undated | Septorhinoplasty €3,200–4,200 | As above |

Naming a clinic here means only that it publishes this information. It is not a ranking or a recommendation. See the [clinics hub](/clinics/) for how clinics are assessed.

## How do the numbers fit together?

Taken together, the sources suggest a range of roughly €2,000–4,500 for primary rhinoplasty and €3,800–5,500 for revision, with press reports lower and one aggregator in the middle. That is a reading of published numbers, not an average of real invoices. Do not budget for the lowest figure.

## How do you read a quote?

Read a quote by checking what it covers, who operates and where, before comparing the total. A good quote is itemised and dated.

1. **Check the operation.** Primary, septorhinoplasty, tip or revision are priced differently. Ask for the technique in words.
2. **Check the surgeon.** Is the surgeon named? Will the person you meet at the consultation be the one operating? See [how to verify a surgeon](/surgeons/how-to-verify/).
3. **Check the setting.** Hospital, day hospital or clinic; anaesthetist on site; nights of stay. See [hospital or clinic](/clinics/hospital-or-clinic/).
4. **Check what is in and out.** Use the list in [what a quote should include](/cost/whats-included/).
5. **Check validity.** How long is the quote valid and in which currency?
6. **Check the extras.** Hotel, flights, tests and medication are often outside the price. See [hidden costs](/cost/hidden-costs/).
7. **Check the terms.** Deposit, cancellation, revision and follow-up after you fly home.

A figure with no scope is not a price. If a clinic or agency will not break it down, that is information.

## Why are prices in Albania lower than in some countries?

Published prices are lower than the ranges Italian press and UK sources report for their own countries, but a price gap is not a measure of quality or of risk. The sources here do not explain the gap and we do not claim to. What matters for you is whether the lower price still includes a qualified surgeon, a properly licensed facility and follow-up.
